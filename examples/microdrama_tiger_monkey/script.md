# Microdrama Script: "शेरू और चीकू" (Sheru & Cheeku)

- **Genre:** 3D Clay Cartoon Comedy (Slice-of-Life / Banter)
- **Visual Style:** Tactile Plasticine Claymation (Aardman & Pixar stop-motion look)
- **Language:** Hindi (Comedic colloquial with Punjabi parody)
- **Continuity Engine:** MiniMax-H3 SatoDive Dual-Seed Routing

---

## 🎬 Shot-by-Shot Visual & Story Plan

### ☀️ SHOT 1: "The Peaceful Morning" (Duration: ~6–7 Seconds)
- **Shot Type:** `WIDE_MASTER_ESTABLISHING` (`wide_shot_present: true`)
- **Action:** 
  Wide sunlit shot of the clay jungle clearing. Sheru the Tiger steps out from the round clay hut in the background, walking upright on his hind legs. He stretches his arms wide, lets out a big lazy morning yawn, walks at a leisurely pace to the wooden table, pulls out his stool, and sits down happily, soaking in the morning breeze.
- **Audio / Dialogue (Sheru):**
  > **"आह... आज का दिन बहुत सुहाना है!"**  
  > *(Aah... aaj ka din bohot suhana hai!)*  
  > *Voice Tone:* Deep, relaxed, peaceful, content.

---

### 🐒 SHOT 2: "The Uninvited Guest" (Duration: ~7–8 Seconds)
- **Shot Type:** `MEDIUM_TWO_SHOT` (`wide_shot_present: false`, `seed_video: Shot 1 Tail`, `seed_ref: Shot 1 Wide`)
- **Action:** 
  Just as Sheru closes his eyes to enjoy the peace, leaves rustle above. Cheeku the Monkey drops down from the tree branches, doing a mid-air flip, and lands right onto the opposite stool with a goofy bounce! Cheeku joins his hands in an overly dramatic Bollywood-style greeting. Sheru’s peaceful smile instantly vanishes into an annoyed, deadpan frown.
- **Audio / Dialogue (Cheeku & Sheru):**
  > **Cheeku (Monkey):**  
  > **"नमस्कार पाजी! तुस्सी की कर रहे हो? बहुत दिनां बाद मिले!"**  
  > *(Namaskar pajji! Tussi ki kar rahe ho? Bohot dina baad mile!)*  
  > *Voice Tone:* High-pitched, enthusiastic, fake Punjabi accent, goofy.  
  >  
  > *(1.2s pause — Sheru glares at him with heavy eyelids)*  
  >  
  > **Sheru (Tiger):**  
  > **"अबे पहले तो... ना तो तू पंजाबी है..."**  
  > *(Abey pehle toh... na toh tu Punjabi hai...)*  
  > *Voice Tone:* Dry, irritated, deadpan, slow.

---

### 🤨 SHOT 3: "The Reality Check" (Duration: ~6–7 Seconds)
- **Shot Type:** `MEDIUM_CLOSE_UP_REVERSE` (`wide_shot_present: false`, `seed_video: Shot 2 Tail`, `seed_ref: Shot 1 Wide`)
- **Action:** 
  Camera pushes in over Cheeku's shoulder to Sheru leaning forward on his paws across the wooden table. Sheru continues his deadpan scolding. Reverse angle shows Cheeku sheepishly scratching the back of his ear, blinking his big cartoon eyes with a mischievous guilty smirk.
- **Audio / Dialogue (Sheru):**
  > **Sheru (Tiger):**  
  > **"...और ना ही तू मेरे से बहुत दिनों बाद मिला है! कल ही तो मिले थे हम!"**  
  > *(...aur na hi tu mere se bohot dino baad mila hai! Kal hi toh mile the hum!)*  
  > *Voice Tone:* Sarcastic, irritated, emphasizing "kal hi to mile the".

---

### 🎁 SHOT 4: "The Sneaky Excuse & The Cliffhanger" (Duration: ~7–8 Seconds)
- **Shot Type:** `MEDIUM_TWO_SHOT_CLIFFHANGER` (`wide_shot_present: false`, `seed_video: Shot 3 Tail`, `seed_ref: Shot 1 Wide`)
- **Action:** 
  Cheeku winks, pulls out a wrapped clay mystery package from behind his back, and slides it across the table. Sheru frowns suspiciously. The package suddenly starts wiggling on its own! Both stare down at it as a strange humming sound starts.
- **Audio / Dialogue (Cheeku):**
  > **Cheeku (Monkey):**  
  > **"अरे वो सब छोड़ो पाजी... ये देखो आपके लिए क्या लाया हूँ!"**  
  > *(Arey woh sab chhodo pajji... yeh dekho aapke liye kya laaya hoon!)*  
  > *Voice Tone:* Playful, teasing, secretive.  
  >  
  > *(Package rattles: "BZZZZZT... TICK... TICK...")*  
  > **Sheru:** *"ये... हिल क्यों रहा है?!"* *(CLIFFHANGER CUT TO BLACK)*
