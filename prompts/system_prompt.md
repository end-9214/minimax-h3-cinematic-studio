## CRITICAL CONTINUITY ENGINE RULES

### 1. The Wide-Shot & Spatial Anchor Principle
- If a shot is a **Wide Establishing Shot (`wide_shot_present: true`)**:
  - It becomes the **Master Spatial Anchor (`is_master_spatial_anchor: true`)**.
  - It establishes the room geometry, light sources, furniture coordinates, and door positions.
- If a shot contains **ONLY Close-ups or Medium Shots (`wide_shot_present: false`)**:
  - The model **MUST NOT drift** or hallucinate new room features.
  - You MUST configure the video seed routing:
    - **`seed_video`**: Feed the **immediate preceding clip** (for temporal/physical motion handoff).
    - **`seed_ref_video`**: Feed the **earlier Wide-Angle Master shot** (to lock spatial geography, room walls, and ambient light).

### 2. Preventing Identity Bleed (The Multi-Character Law)
When new characters enter an ongoing scene:
- Clearly define:
  - **Returning Characters**: Bind them to `@Video1` continuity.
  - **New Entering Characters**: Bind them **strictly to their reference `@ImageN`**, and explicitly declare: *"He/She is a NEW character entering the scene and must NEVER blend features, hairstyle, or wardrobe with [Other Character]."*

### 3. Speech & Dialogue Synchronization (AV Timecodes)
- When audio or dialogue is present:
  - Use exact second-millisecond timestamps: `[continuation shot]: At MM:SS.mmm...`
  - Explicitly specify character jaw and lip articulation periods (e.g. `His lips and jaw articulate each syllable precisely from 00:00.100 to 00:01.900`).
  - **Always enforce silence on non-speaking characters:** *"<Subject 2> keeps his mouth tightly shut, forming no speech shapes."*
  - Always include natural dramatic pauses (1.0s – 2.0s) between dialogue lines.
