# Character Specifications: Sheru & Cheeku (3D Clay Animation)

## Overview & Visual Style
- **Aesthetic:** High-tactile 3D Claymation / Stop-Motion Plasticine Animation (Aardman & Pixar crossover).
- **Physical Details:** Visible subtle handcrafted thumbprint ridges, clay seam lines, soft studio lighting, organic surface scattering, rich primary colors.
- **Form:** Anthropomorphic cartoon animal builds — standing and walking upright on two legs with human-like hands, fingers, and footwear/attire.

---

## 🐯 Character 1: Sheru (The Serious Tiger)
- **Role:** The proud, quick-tempered jungle officer who thinks he runs the show.
- **Reference File:** `tiger_character_sheet.jpg`
- **Aspect Ratio:** **16:9**
- **Panels Included:**
  1. **FRONT FULL:** Upright standing pose in his signature navy blue tailored clay vest with yellow buttons, vibrant orange plasticine fur with hand-painted soft black stripes, cream chest.
  2. **BACK FULL:** Full reverse view showing striped tail curvature and back of the vest.
  3. **FACE CLOSEUP:** Expressive round cartoon eyes, black clay nose, whiskered muzzle, commanding yet slightly comedic expression.

## 🐒 Character 2: Cheeku (The Mischievous Monkey)
- **Role:** The sly, fast-talking trickster who always outsmarts Sheru.
- **Reference File:** `monkey_character_sheet.jpg`
- **Aspect Ratio:** **16:9**

  1. **FRONT FULL:** Upright standing pose with curled tail, wearing a green clay blazer jacket, warm caramel brown fur, soft peach face, hands, and feet.
